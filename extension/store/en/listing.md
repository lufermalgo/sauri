# Sauri

## Short description
Record any browser tab and send it to Sauri on your Mac for transcripts and summaries. No sign-in required.

## Detailed description
Record audio and video from a Chrome tab and review your meetings in Sauri on your Mac.

Choose a tab to record, include your microphone when needed, and control recording from the side panel. Pause, resume, and stop recording. A recording notice is shown in Google Meet.

Sauri on your Mac receives the recording to transcribe it, identify speakers, and summarize it. The extension connects automatically without asking for an email or password. Requires the Sauri app installed on a Mac with Apple silicon.

If the app is closed, the panel asks you to open it. Pending recordings remain in this browser and are sent automatically when the app responds. Do not uninstall the extension or clear its data while recordings are pending.

To start, open Sauri on your Mac, click the extension icon, and choose a tab to record. Allow microphone access to include your voice. Stop recording when finished and review the result in Sauri.

Recordings are temporarily stored in your browser and sent to the local app, not to an external server. The extension contains no analytics and requires no account. Inform participants and obtain any required permission before recording.
